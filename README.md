Echo – Web Player: Music for Everyone

Echo – A sleek and interactive web-based music player that allows users to explore, play, and manage their favorite songs. With a visually engaging interface, users can browse tracks, control playback, and enjoy music seamlessly in their browser.

____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
📖 About

Echo is a modern, browser-based music platform designed to provide an immersive listening experience. It features sections like Recently Played, Trending Now, and Featured Charts, helping users discover both popular and personal favorites. Each song card displays album artwork, title, singer, and additional info, while the footer music player shows real-time progress, song details, and controls for play, pause, next, previous, and random playback. Echo combines simplicity with interactive functionality, making music browsing and listening effortless.

____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
✨ Features

🎵 Dynamic Playback – Click on a song to play instantly; automatically stops previous track to prevent overlap.

🎧 Footer Music Player – Play/pause, next, previous, and random song controls with live progress bar.

🖼️ Song Details – Displays album art, song title, singer, and total/current playback time.

🔄 Navigation – Circular next/previous song playback and random track selection.

🗂️ Interactive Cards – Visually appealing card-based layout for browsing songs.

🌍 Responsive Design – Works seamlessly across devices.

💻 Frontend Only – Built entirely with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Download or clone the repository.
